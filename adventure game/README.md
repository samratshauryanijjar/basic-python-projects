# Jungle Adventure Game 🌴

A simple text-based adventure game built in Python.

The player enters their name and then makes choices to explore a jungle, cross a bridge, enter a cave, and solve a riddle to find a hidden treasure.

## How It Works

The game gives you different paths and choices throughout the adventure.

### Main Paths

- 🌉 Cross the bridge
  - Sprint across the bridge
  - Crawl across the bridge
  - Find an altar and solve a riddle

- 🌿 Pass through the vines
  - Enter the glowing tunnel
  - Enter the dark tunnel
  - Find another altar and solve the same riddle

Choosing the correct answers can lead you to the jungle's lost treasure.
